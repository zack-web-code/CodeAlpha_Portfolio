About Me

I'm a Business Information Technology student at the University of Johannesburg, with a strong interest in frontend development, business analysis and creating meaningful digital solutions.

My experience working on software projects has taught me that developing a successful application involves more than writing code. It requires creativity, collaboration, understanding user needs and finding practical ways to solve problems.

I'm continuously developing my technical skills while exploring how technology can help businesses operate more effectively and improve people's everyday experiences.

Beyond Writing Code

I believe the most valuable solutions begin with understanding the problem.

My interest in technology goes beyond building websites and applications. I'm interested in understanding how businesses operate, identifying challenges they experience and developing solutions that address their actual needs.

This approach influences how I think about software development. Rather than simply building something because I can, I want to understand why it needs to exist and what value it will provide.

My Journey

Every journey has its turning points. Mine taught me that progress isn't always a straight line.

My educational journey has been filled with challenges, difficult decisions and opportunities to start again. What began as a struggle to find direction eventually became a journey of personal growth, determination and academic achievement.

  1. Kokstad

    Where It Began

    My high school journey started at Carl Malcomess High School in KwaZulu-Natal. During this period, I struggled academically because I hadn't yet developed a clear vision for my education.

  2. 2018

    A New Beginning in Durban

    Following my grandmother's encouragement, I moved to Wentworth, Durban, where I repeated Grade 10 at Fairvale. Unfortunately, I experienced another academic setback.

  3. 2019

    A Year Away

    I took a gap year, stepping away from formal education.

  4. 2020–2021

    Johannesburg

    I enrolled at Destiny Charter Schools, where I studied Commerce and successfully passed Grade 10. However, I left school again during Grade 11 in 2021 because I still hadn't discovered my purpose.

  5. 2022

    Another Year Away

    I remained out of school, still searching for direction and a clearer sense of purpose.

  6. 2023

    The Turning Point

    Encouraged by my mother, I returned to school at Mangwazana High School in Obonjeni, KwaZulu-Natal, where I enrolled in Grade 11. This time, I approached education with a different mindset, greater determination and a renewed sense of purpose. It marked the beginning of a significant transformation in my life.

  7. 2024

    The Achievement

    I completed Grade 12 at Mangwazana High School, achieving six distinctions and finishing as the school's top-performing student. This achievement represented more than academic success. It was proof of how far I had come through perseverance, personal growth and a change in mindset.

  8. 2025–Present

    University of Johannesburg

    Today, I'm studying towards a Diploma in Business Information Technology at my dream university, pursuing a field I've developed a genuine passion for.

My journey has taught me that your past does not have to define your future. What matters is your willingness to grow, change and keep moving forward.
